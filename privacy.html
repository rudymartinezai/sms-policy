Privacy Policy

Rodolfo Martinez (sole proprietor) · Last updated October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers the SMS reminder program run by Rodolfo Martinez. The program sends personal reminder and to-do text messages only to Rodolfo Martinez's own mobile phone. No messages are sent to any other person.

Information we collect

The only information used is the recipient's mobile phone number and the text of the reminders being sent. That number belongs to Rodolfo Martinez, who gave verbal consent to receive these messages.

How information is used

The phone number is used only to deliver reminder text messages through our messaging provider, Twilio. It is not used for marketing.

SMS opt-in data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or consent with any third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the messaging providers needed to deliver the messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.

Changes

Rodolfo Martinez may update this policy. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
